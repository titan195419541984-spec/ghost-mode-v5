Privacy at Ghost Mode Studio

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Prompts and generated media

When you submit a prompt, the prompt and selected options are sent to the configured third-party model provider to process the request. Generated media may be returned through that provider's infrastructure. Do not enter passwords, payment details, private records, confidential business information, or other sensitive information.

Accounts and history

Ghost Mode Studio does not currently offer user accounts or persistent prompt history. Video job status is held temporarily in the running server process while a request is handled. Providers may process or retain requests under their own terms and privacy practices.

Third-party services

Image and video generation depend on external model providers. Their availability, data handling, and retention practices are controlled by those providers. Review relevant provider policies before submitting content.

Advertising

Advertising placements are not currently displayed in the studio interface while the site is being prepared for review. If advertising is enabled later, this policy should be updated before those technologies are used.
